Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information AIM-Ami ("we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. You can reach us at helloaimami@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Contact and booking forms: your name, company, phone number, email address, subject, and message.
  • Newsletter: your email address.
  • Technical data: basic details your browser shares with the sites that host and serve this page, such as IP address and device type.
  • Email checks: when you submit an email address, your browser looks up the address's domain, not the full address, with a public DNS service to catch typos and fake domains.
  • Your browser storage: we use session and local storage only to remember that you have seen or completed the newsletter pop-up. It is not used to track you across other sites.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and deliver the services you request.
  • To send newsletter emails if you subscribe.
  • To keep the website secure and improve it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our form and email providers, scheduling and payment tools [add the tools you use], and our website host. They may only use it to provide their service to us. Links to YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide services, meet legal obligations, and run the newsletter. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and we will honor valid requests.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We will never ask you to send passwords through this website.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email helloaimami@gmail.com.
